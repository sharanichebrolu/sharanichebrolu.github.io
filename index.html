About the initiative

Technology is becoming part of childhood earlier than ever. We believe digital education should begin just as early.

Sharani Chebrolu and Ammar co-founded the Digital Safety & AI Workshop Initiative to make technology education engaging, accessible, and relevant for younger students.

Through classroom discussions, games, demonstrations, and collaborative activities, students explore online safety, artificial intelligence, computer science, and technology careers.

As Co-Founder and Director, Sharani leads curriculum development, communication with schools, workshop planning, outreach, website development, and the initiative’s continued expansion.

OUR MISSION

Give children the knowledge and confidence to make thoughtful decisions online.

We transform complicated technology concepts into lessons that are interactive, understandable, and meaningful for young students.

02

What students learn

01

Digital Safety

Students learn how to protect personal information, recognize unsafe situations, create stronger passwords, and make responsible choices online.

02

Artificial Intelligence

Students explore what AI is, where they encounter it, how it learns from information, and why human judgment still matters.

03

Computer Science

Activities introduce ideas such as binary, problem-solving, instructions, and how computers process information.

04

Technology Careers

Students discover the variety of people and careers involved in creating, protecting, and improving technology.
